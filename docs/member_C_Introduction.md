## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background & Goal

Finding affordable housing in San Diego can be challenging for UCSD students, especially for those who just moved to San Diego and are unfamiliar with the local rental market. Our project aims 
to analyze rental price differences across San Diego neighborhoods and explore how factors such as location, apartment size, number of bedrooms, and amenities affect rent.

We plan to use publicly available housing data from sources such as Zillow Research, Apartments.com, and the U.S. Census Bureau, etc... By cleaning and analyzing the data, we hope to help 
UCSD students compare rental prices and make the most suitable renting decision.

### 1.2 In Scope

Our project will focus on the following tasks:

- Collect and clean rental housing data near UC San Diego.
- Compare rental prices across ZIP codes and unit sizes.
- Calculate median rent, quartiles, and rent per square foot when data are available.
- Create at least three visualizations showing rental price differences and trends.
- Allow users to filter rental listings by monthly budget and number of bedrooms.
- Export matching rental listings and provide a reproducible analysis workflow.

### 1.3 Out of Scope

The following features are not included in our project:

- Predicting future rental prices using advanced machine learning models.
- Providing real-time rental availability or continuously updating listings.
- Allowing users to book apartments or contact landlords through our project.
- Analyzing housing markets outside San Diego county.
- Providing personalized housing recommendations beyond the available filtering options.
